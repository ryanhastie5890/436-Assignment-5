How to play

Classic tic-tac-toe. X goes first. Pick NEW-GAME to wait for opponent, or JOIN to accept someone's waiting game.

  1. Choose a screen name and SUBMIT.
  2. Click NEW-GAME and pick X or O to wait for opponent.
  3. Other clients can JOIN your waiting slot.
  4. When the game starts, X moves first. Click an empty cell to play.
